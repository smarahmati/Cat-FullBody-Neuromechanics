Exact dynamic topology and numeric mechanical arrays are included. The 21 coordinates describe sagittal progression, height, trunk pitch and limb/girdle motion. Detailed bone surfaces are optional visualization assets and never enter body inertia or contact.

The public package uses schematic bone visuals generated from the mechanical model. Optional detailed surfaces under Model/meshes are not distributed because redistribution permission is not established. Visual meshes do not define the simulated inertias, joint constraints or paw-contact law.

Visual sources: SimTK Cat Hindlimb and Fujiwara (2018). Registration, left-side mirroring and articulation are project adaptations. Downloading the original archives alone does not reproduce the prepared mesh registration; prepared anatomical surfaces require registration to this model.
