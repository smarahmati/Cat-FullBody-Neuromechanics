The Intact_Walking folder contains the complete 60-second forward calculation from the supplied excitation and model parameters. No body fall was detected over this interval.

The simulation predicts activation, muscle–tendon forces, joint and body motion, muscle moments and raw paw-contact forces. Experimental time series are used for comparison, not to impose the motion or GRFs.
